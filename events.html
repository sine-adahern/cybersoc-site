The Story So Far

The MTU Cybersecurity Society went from dormant to active in a matter of months with an AGM and relaunch, CTF training, a mention at the MTU Society Awards, and members on a panel at a global telecoms conference. Scroll down to walk through the timeline.

Feb 2026

AGM & Relaunch

The society is officially active again. After almost a year of inactivity, we held our AGM to elect a board and set out our plans for the semester and beyond. From CTFs to HackSpaces to industry talks. It was here that I was elected Chairperson, becoming the first ever first-year to be elected to such a prestigious position. The turnout and engagement on the night confirmed what we'd suspected: the appetite for this community had been there all along.

March 2026

CTF Training Night & Mini Competition

Meeting new members where they are. Capture the Flag competitions are one of the best on-ramps into practical security work but they're intimidating if nobody has ever explained them to you. So we ran a session that did exactly that: what a CTF actually is, how to approach the different challenge categories, and which resources and platforms members could use to keep practising on their own. We then put the theory straight into practice with our own mini CTF competition, so everyone in the room got to solve something themselves before they went home.

March 2026

MTU Society Awards

A young society gets a mention. A great night for the society, and a real marker of progress. It was brilliant to see the range of societies across MTU turn out and, as one of the newest societies in the room, to be mentioned at all. It was equally valuable as inspiration: seeing what more established societies had built gave us a clear sense of what we could aim for next.

June 2026

ROCCO Genesis 2026, Limerick

Our members on a global stage. Society members attended ROCCO Genesis 2026, a global telecoms conference held in Limerick.

Speaking on the "Human & Artificial Intelligence" panel. As Chairperson, I was invited to sit on the panel alongside leaders from across the industry, exploring a question at the heart of our field: as AI becomes more capable, what is the irreplaceable role of human intelligence?

Competing in the "Trust the Signal" hackathon. Our members took on a real-world security challenge; how do we build trust into SMS and voice communication? With mentorship from the teams at Verizon and Cellusys.

Beyond the panel and the hackathon, the conference put our members in the same room as students from universities across Ireland and industry leaders from around the world. Watching the society work with (and occasionally against 😅) each other, and seeing how far we'd come in only a few months, was the clearest proof yet that rebuilding this was worth doing.

Onwards and we can't wait to see what we take on next.
