MTU Cybersecurity Society

Cybersecurity Society at MTU is a community for students interested in cybersecurity, ethical hacking, offensive security, and cryptography. Join us to learn, collaborate, and explore the world of digital security.

About Us

MTU is one of Ireland's leading cybersecurity hubs, home to Cork's Nimbus Research Centre and its national Cyber Range and prototyping labs. Through dedicated degrees and the €7M Cyber Innovate programme, MTU is training the next generation of cybersecurity innovators. The MTU Cybersecurity Society brings that world to students with hands-on skills, CTFs, and real connections to Ireland's cyber industry. At its heart, the society is about people; creating a space where students connect, collaborate, and grow together, with no experience required. From beginners to seasoned hackers, we're a community built on curiosity, teamwork, and a shared drive to learn. Join us, and help shape the next generation of cyber talent at MTU.
